Short description:
Snake game with 16 modes — from retro Nokia to roguelike dungeon crawler

Full description:
Naga is the ultimate snake game — 16 unique game modes packed into one app, ranging from the classic Nokia-style retro experience to a full roguelike dungeon crawler.

GAME MODES
- Classic: The original snake experience, just like you remember it.
- Retro Nokia: Faithful recreation of the iconic mobile game.
- Speed Run: Race against the clock — eat as fast as you can.
- Endless: No walls, no limits. How long can you survive?
- Maze: Navigate through increasingly complex labyrinths.
- Portal: Teleporters warp you across the board.
- Invisible: The snake disappears — trust your memory.
- Mirror: Controls are reversed. Left is right, up is down.
- Split: Your snake splits into two — control both at once.
- Growth: Every fruit makes you grow twice as fast.
- Shrink: Eat special fruit to shrink back down.
- Battle: Compete head-to-head on one device.
- Poison: Avoid the bad fruit mixed in with the good.
- Time Warp: Time slows and speeds unpredictably.
- Gravity: The snake falls — platformer meets snake.
- Dungeon: A full roguelike — explore rooms, fight enemies, collect loot.

FEATURES
- 16 distinct game modes with unique mechanics
- Smooth, responsive touch and swipe controls
- Dark retro-inspired visual design
- Local high score tracking for every mode
- Works offline — no internet required after install
- No ads, no accounts, no data collection
- Runs on Android, iOS, and web

CONTROLS
- Swipe in any direction to steer
- Tap to start or pause
- Simple, intuitive, and responsive
