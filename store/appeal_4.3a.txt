SCOPE AND ORIGINALITY

Naga is roughly 18,300 lines of Dart, about 11,000 of it per-mode gameplay logic. Its 19 modes are genuinely different games rather than palette swaps of one loop — each is a separate game class with its own rules, state model, and even its own notion of time:

- Dungeon is a turn-based roguelike with procedurally generated rooms, monsters that act only after the player moves, traps, descending floors, and an inventory of swords, arrows, wall-breaking hammers and shields. It advances one step per keypress and has no real-time tick at all.
- Naga Dive is a continuous-physics side-scroller with gravity, flap impulse and terminal velocity.
- Fangs is a Breakout variant with ball physics and destructible blocks.
- Venom is a Bomberman-style mode with fused bombs, radial blast propagation and a five-level campaign.
- VS AI offers four difficulty tiers backed by a real AI implementation: breadth-first pathfinding with tail-reachability verification and flood-fill space evaluation, falling back to longest-path tail-following when no safe route to food exists.
- Stampede is a five-lane racer, Trail a Tron light-cycle mode, Maze Hunter a pursuit mode with pathfinding ghosts, and Snake II, ASCII, CGA and Nibbles distinct recreations of different historical styles.

The app also has a configurable rules system, five power-up types, achievements, local high scores, per-mode music, and English and German localisation.

NO SPAM INCENTIVE

Naga is free, with no advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, accounts, analytics, tracking or data collection. It needs no network access and works fully offline. No monetisation path exists that submitting duplicative apps could serve.
