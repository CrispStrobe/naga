Naga is not one Snake game with 27 skins. It is 27 different games that start from the same idea (steer a growing serpent) and then change the rules.

RULES NO OTHER SNAKE GAME HAS
• Ouroboros: fireflies can't be eaten. Catch them by closing a loop of your own body around them. More fireflies in one loop score far more.
• Echo: your last run comes back as a ghost that retraces every move. Outlast your own past.
• Shed: every fourth meal, your old skin stays behind as solid wall. Lay it along a row to clear it.
• Territory: leave your land to draw a trail, come home to claim everything it encloses, and race two AI rivals to half the jungle.
• Daily Serpent: one board per day, identical on every phone, tablet, Mac and browser. Play it again and again and build a streak.
• Nightfall: only a lantern lights the board, and it dims as your score climbs.
• Portals: a linked pair of portals that jumps somewhere new after every meal.

WHOLE GENRES, REBUILT AROUND A SNAKE
• A turn-based roguelike dungeon with monsters, traps, arrows, hammers and shields
• A bomb-and-blast campaign with chain reactions across five levels
• A brick breaker where your snake is the paddle
• A maze chase against pathfinding ghosts
• A light-cycle arena, an underwater swim, a five-lane animal race and last-snake-standing survival

FAITHFUL RETRO
An LCD-phone classic, a green-on-black terminal drawn in real ASCII, four-colour CGA, a 1991 DOS classic with its maze levels, and more.

TOGETHER OR AGAINST THE MACHINE
Duel a friend on one device, or take on AI opponents at four difficulty levels, driven by real pathfinding rather than random moves.

MADE WITH RESPECT FOR YOU
Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking and no data collection. Works fully offline. The complete source code is open at github.com/CrispStrobe/naga.

Available in English and German.
