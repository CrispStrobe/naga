Resubmission of Naga after the 4.3(a) review of version 1.0. Thank you for taking another look.

Naga is an original app, written from scratch. Its full source code and history are public at https://github.com/CrispStrobe/naga, where every claim below can be checked. It uses no template, purchased code, or white-label SDK.

Since 1.0 we have added seven game modes with rules we believe no other snake game on the App Store has, each a separate game class:
- Ouroboros: fireflies can only be caught by enclosing them in a loop of your own body (a flood fill decides what is enclosed; walls never count).
- Echo: your previous run replays as a ghost snake you must avoid.
- Shed: every fourth meal the body behind the head becomes permanent wall; full rows of it clear.
- Territory: a land-claiming game against two AI rivals.
- Daily Serpent: one seeded board per day, identical on every platform.
- Nightfall and Portals: a lantern-lit board, and a portal pair that moves after every meal.

The other 20 modes include a turn-based roguelike, a bomb campaign, a brick breaker and a maze chase with pathfinding ghosts. The screenshots show eight different modes.

How to test: no account or login is needed and the app works fully offline. From the home screen, tap any section header to expand it, then tap a mode. The (i) button in a game explains its rules.

The app has no ads, in-app purchases, accounts, analytics or data collection, and makes no network requests.
