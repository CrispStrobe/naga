Naga — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Naga is a snake game that runs entirely on your device. Your privacy is simple: we don't collect anything.

Data Collection

Naga does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No user accounts or sign-ins
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No crash reporting sent to external servers

Local Storage

High scores and game settings are stored locally on your device using your browser's or platform's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device.

Network Requests

Naga makes no network requests beyond the initial loading of the application itself. Once loaded, the game functions entirely offline.

Third Parties

Naga does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Naga is suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected from anyone, no special provisions for children are necessary.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at postmaster@crispstro.be.
